## Brand & Style

This design system occupies the intersection of an international architectural publication and an haute interior design atelier. The voice is contemplative, erudite, and disciplined—prioritizing spatial luxury, curated material selections, and artisanal tactility.

### Personality & Emotional Response
- **Quiet Authority:** Confident, deliberate compositions that never shout. Visual balance replaces decorative embellishment.
- **Atmospheric Warmth:** Rooted in grounded minerals, sun-dried stone, hand-troweled plaster, and patinated metals.
- **Architectural Clarity:** Strict structural alignments evocative of bespoke joinery and editorial layout grids.
- **Sensory Resonance:** Evoking unpolished limestone, heavy linen weaves, and raw brass through subtle tonal relationships.

### Design Movement & Aesthetic
- **Warm Editorial Minimalism with Tactile Restraint:** Expansive negative space acts as a core structural element. Interfaces are framed like gallery spaces: generous borders, razor-thin hairline dividers, and deliberate type hierarchy. Elevation is created through subtle tonal shifts rather than heavy drop shadows.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from architectural materials: raw linen, hand-cut stone, patinated brass, deep umber wood, and weathered olive leaves.

### Primary Hierarchy
- **Canvas (`#F5F2EC`):** Warm Ivory provides the primary ambient surface, mimicking heavy untreated cotton paper or limestone.
- **Structural Neutral (`#242321`):** Deep Charcoal delivers sharp legibility for headings, foundational text, and high-priority glyphs without the stark harshness of pure black.
- **Primary Accent (`#3A3028`):** Deep Umber Brown is reserved for key interactions, solid structural buttons, and active navigational tiers.
- **Secondary Accent (`#B49A6A`):** Brushed Gold provides an artisanal glint for elevated badges, selection states, and delicate metadata tags.
- **Tertiary Accent (`#73715F`):** Muted Olive serves secondary status states, subtle iconography, and botanical architectural notes.
- **Stone Tint (`#D8D2C8`):** Serves as surface layering, card fills, and delicate hairline separators (`1px solid`).

## Typography

The typographic system pairs the high-contrast elegance of **Playfair Display** with the restrained architectural geometry of **Manrope**.

### Usage Guidelines
- **Headlines & Display:** Playfair Display set in Regular (400) weight creates editorial posture. Never set Playfair in full bold; weight contrast is achieved through scale rather than thickness.
- **Body:** Manrope delivers neutral, balanced legibility across long-form essays, project specifications, and architectural documentation.
- **Labels & Microcopy:** Small caps and extended letter-spacing (`0.08em` to `0.12em`) transform functional meta tags, captions, and section indicators into rhythmic architectural annotations.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an editorial grid reminiscent of broadsheet magazines and monograph folios.

### Grid Structure
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column grid, 48px canvas margins, 32px gutters. Asymmetric spans (e.g., 5-column editorial text flanking a 7-column architectural elevation) are preferred over symmetrical layouts.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column grid, 32px canvas margins, 24px gutters.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column grid, 20px canvas margins, 16px gutters.

### Editorial Rhythm
Generous whitespace functions as a functional element, lending breathing room to high-resolution imagery and physical materials. Vertical pacing between sections defaults to `space-xl` (64px) on desktop to preserve an unhurried, galleried tempo.

## Elevation & Depth

Spatial layering avoids heavy drop shadows, relying instead on structural surface differentiation and refined micro-outlines.

### Surface Hierarchy
- **Base Canvas:** `#F5F2EC` (Warm Ivory).
- **Surface Elevation 1 (Card & Module Inset):** `#FAF8F5` or filled `#D8D2C8` with opacity overlays (`rgba(216, 210, 200, 0.45)`).
- **Surface Elevation 2 (Flyouts & Floating Panels):** Pure warm white `#FAF9F6` framed with a 1px perimeter border in `#D8D2C8`.

### Outlines & Borders
- Structural boundaries and dividers use razor hairlines: `1px solid #D8D2C8`.
- For interactive focus or elevated demarcation, use a `1px solid #B49A6A` hairline accent.

### Shadows
Shadows are minimized and tinted with ambient umber rather than neutral grey:
- **Floating Modals & Curated Trays:** `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(58, 48, 40, 0.08)`.

## Shapes

The design system embraces an architectural rectilinear geometry (`roundedness: 0`). 

Corners are sharp and uncompromising, reinforcing the discipline of drafting tables, floor plans, and bespoke carpentry. Cards, image containers, input fields, and buttons have clean right angles (0px radius). Circular forms are strictly reserved for media player controls and directional pagination indicators.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#3A3028`, text `#F5F2EC`, sharp edges (0px radius). Padding: 14px 28px. Font: `label-lg` in uppercase with subtle letter-spacing. Hover state transitions to `#242321`.
- **Secondary (Hairline):** Background transparent, border `1px solid #3A3028`, text `#3A3028`. Hover: background fills with `rgba(58, 48, 40, 0.05)`.
- **Text Link:** Text `#242321`, underlined with a `1px solid #B49A6A` rule set 4px below baseline. Hover: line color shifts to `#3A3028`.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs:** Background transparent or `#FAF9F6`, bottom border only (`1px solid #D8D2C8`) or full 1px hairline box. Padding: 12px 16px. Font: `body-md`. Focus state transitions the border to `#B49A6A` with no default browser glow.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Sharp square boxes (0px radius) framed in `1px solid #3A3028`. Selected states feature a solid `#3A3028` fill with an ivory inner indicator.

### Cards & Artifact Display
- **Portfolio Cards:** Sharp corners, borderless or framed in `1px solid #D8D2C8`. Imagery is primary; project classifications sit above the title in uppercase `label-sm` with `#73715F`.
- **Material Swatch Module:** Clean grid cells featuring high-fidelity architectural finish samples, paired with mono-spaced numeric references and `#B49A6A` gold leaf accents.

### Chips & Meta Badges
- Non-rounded rectilinear containers. Background `rgba(216, 210, 200, 0.35)`, border `1px solid #D8D2C8`. Text `#3A3028` in `label-sm`.

### Architectural Specific Components
- **Spec Sheet Drawer:** Clean, sliding right-hand slate with `1px solid #D8D2C8` left divider, cataloging material composition, artisan origin, and dimensional metrics.
- **Editorial Pull Quote:** Oversized Playfair Display quote paired with an indented hairline border in Brushed Gold.
