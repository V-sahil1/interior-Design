Materiality & Specimen

The Earth House — Design Study

“Designed Around Quiet Moments.”

In an era of hyper-synthetic finishes, we turned to vernacular geology. By using rammed earth mixed from regional red silt alongside honed Dholpur sandstone, the interior functions as a self-regulating thermal sink—naturally dropping interior ambient temperatures by 4°C during Gujarat’s dry summer peaks.

Methodology

The Design Process

From first idea to final detail: a five-stage collaborative discipline.

Stage 01

Discover

Understanding lifestyle rituals, spatial light orientation, family requirements, and functional intentions before drawing a line.

Stage 02

Concept

Developing tactile moodboards, volumetric sketches, hand-drawn spatial flows, and architectural material narratives.

Stage 03

Design

Precise CAD architectural drafting, photorealistic 3D light studies, millwork joinery details, and full MEP electrical plans.

Stage 04

Execute

Rigorous on-site governance, artisanal stone cutting, custom joinery fabrication, and precise structural monitoring.

Stage 05

Reveal

Turnkey staging, bespoke linen placement, hand-selected art curation, and a serene personal handover.
